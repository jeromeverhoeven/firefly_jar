# Asset: 6080015

## Description
Development note defining the "Courtship" flash sequence: a male *Photinus pyralis* J-flash, faked with a lower-to-higher LED hand-off, and a dim female answer from the lowest LED. Includes field data with sources, the exact timeline, rules, proposed firmware constants, pseudocode, and open items for reconciling with the flash pattern simulator ([6080014](./6080014.md)).
