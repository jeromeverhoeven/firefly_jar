# Asset: 6080014

## Description
Interactive simulator ("Big Dipper Dialogue") of a scripted *Photinus pyralis* courtship story played on the jar's six LEDs (Rev D); Rev C's free-running model is described below: five males (LEDs 1-5) patrolling with J-flashes on a temperature-set cadence, the female (LED 6) answering the male with the longest flash, his approach one LED at a time, landing and ground dialogue, rival males converging, then rest and a new evening. Includes a six-lane flash scope, an event log, and an export of the firmware constants for [Spec-2's Data Model](../Design/Spec-2.md#data-model-firmware-configuration) and the [courtship dev note](./DevNote-Courtship-Sequence.md). Supports tuning [DD-9](../Design/DD-9-Glow-Pattern.md)'s glow-pattern parameters before they're committed to firmware.

Published artifact: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f9e94bdf-678f-432e-8ac6-cef761c03a79

## Revision
- A: initial build — male/female flash envelopes, dip-and-hook flight path, homing/"locating" approach over cycles, oscilloscope with documented-range bands, temperature scaling (illustrative), firmware constant export.
- B: phone layout fix — taller square stage with playback controls moved below the jar at ≤680 px width (previously the controls covered the jar), canvas text/LEDs scaled for high-density screens, and the firmware code box no longer forces sideways scrolling.
- C: rebuilt as a six-LED courtship model from the 2026-10-12 field-literature review — per-male flash lengths (0.4-0.8 s) with female choice of the longest; cadence from Edmunds 1963 (5.9 s at 24 °C, Q10 1.85, long-tailed jitter); J-flash as an LED hand-off; female delay scaled with temperature (assumed); approach, ground dialogue, rival convergence ("love knot"), rest/reset; six-lane scope, event log, Restart and 4x speed.
- D: scripted courtship story per JP (2026-10-12) — three males on LEDs 1-3 at 4/5/6 s (dominant 6 s by default, switchable), rivals 0.4 s flashes, dominant 0.8 s + 0.25 s extra fade; 8 steps: patrol (3 dominant cycles), female answers (2), rival drops to LED 4 and she goes quiet (3 rival cycles), rival returns (3), she answers again (3), dominant descends to LED 4 (3), lands on LED 5 (2), pair dark while rivals flash (5), loop. Each step ends after the female's answer to the last counted flash. Story panel, J dip removed, temperature control replaced by fixed story cadences.
- E: per JP (2026-10-12) — step 4 shortened to one dominant cycle; the female's answer (0.51 s) now always starts 0.54 s after the dominant male's onset so it ends exactly when his 1.05 s flash-and-fade ends (replaces the ~2 s field delay and its slider); firmware export gains FEMALE_FLASH_MS / FEMALE_ONSET_MS.
