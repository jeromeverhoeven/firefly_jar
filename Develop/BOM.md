## Sourcing notes
- **LEDs, jars, and sheet goods are bulk-only.** The eBay LED listing, both jar vendors, the plywood, and the vellum are sold only in fixed packs (100, 12, 12, 20, and 100 respectively) — there is no distributor "qty 1" or "qty 10" price break to quote, so the Cost columns show the pack price and the effective per-item cost it works out to.
- **R7 and R1-R6 pricing is an estimate** (marked "est."): DigiKey's own qty-10 break for this exact YAGEO CFR-25 value wasn't confirmed by automated search in this pass — confirm the live price on the DigiKey product pages linked above before ordering.
- **PET jar vendor is a substitute**, not the one named in [DD-10](../Design/DD-10-Enclosure.md) — the Cornucopia 16 oz 8-pack DD-10 names was out of stock as of Oct 9, 2026. This ZMYBCPACK 12-pack is a same-category replacement found during this BOM pass. **Per DD-10's own open item, test-fit one jar's thread against the regular-mouth lid disc/band before ordering the rest.**
- **Jar split:** DD-10 assumes a 6/6 PET-vs-glass split pending the actual cousin age count — order rows 8 and 9 in that ratio until the age split (an [open item](../Design/Spec-2.md#open-items) in Spec-2) is confirmed.
- **Shared consumables** (moss, glue, wire kit, and the fractional plywood/vellum sheets) are bought once and divided across all 12 units by hand during assembly — their Qty column says "shared" rather than a per-jar count.
- **Spares:** order the 100-pack of LEDs even though only 72 are needed (6 x 12) — this already includes 28 spares per [DD-4](../Design/DD-4-LED-Type-and-Count.md). No other line needs a separate spares order at this batch size.
