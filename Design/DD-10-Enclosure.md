# Design Decision: Enclosure (Glass vs. Shatter-Resistant Jar)

**Asset:** [6080012](./6080012.md)

## Decision
What jar should each cousin get: glass, shatter-resistant PET, or a mix by age? The design asks kids to tap and shake the jar ([DD-1](./DD-1-Trigger-Mechanism.md)), which puts [MuSCoW](../Define/MuSCoW.md) item 15 (safety, *Must*) and item 17 (durability, *Must*) in direct tension with a glass jar.

## Criteria
- **Item 15, safety (Must):** a 7-year-old shaking or dropping the jar must not create a laceration hazard.
- **Item 17, durability (Must):** survives being knocked over and a drop from table height onto carpet, for years.
- **Item 1, firefly-in-a-jar look (Must)** and **item 14, audience 7-16 (Must):** teens must not find it babyish; real glass reads as a "real" keepsake.
- **Item 12, maximum lasting joy (Should):** heft and clarity matter to how special it feels.
- **Compatibility with [DD-7](./DD-7-LED-Stem-Mounting.md):** the same lid disc and screw band (regular-mouth thread) must fit every jar.
- **Items 3 and 16:** cost and domestic availability.

## Discussion / Research
**Glass mason jars.**
- Ball regular-mouth 16 oz pint jars cost **$13.44 per 12-pack, ~$1.12 each** ([HEB](https://www.heb.com/product-detail/ball-regular-mouth-12-pk-pint-mason-jars-lids/30016)).
- Best clarity and heft, and they read as authentic.
- Glass usually survives a short drop onto carpet, but not onto hardwood or tile. A cracked jar near a 7-year-old fails item 15.

**PET "plastic mason" jars.**
- Shatterproof, BPA-free PET pints exist with mason styling, for example [Cornucopia 16 oz 8-pack](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Cornucopia-Brands-16oz-Clear-Plastic-Mason-Jars-8-Pack-Gold-Metal-Lids-PET-BPA-Free-Mason-Style-w-One-Piece-Lids-2-Cup-Pint-Capacity/5082505237) at $24.99, ~$3.12 each, but **out of stock** as of Oct 9, 2026.
- Many PET mason-style jars ship with **one-piece lids**. Their thread must accept a standard **regular-mouth band** for the [DD-7](./DD-7-LED-Stem-Mounting.md) lid disc. **Verify before ordering** by asking the seller or test-fitting one jar.
- Commercial PET wide-mouth jars (e.g., [Uline](https://www.uline.com/BL_620/Clear-PET-Round-Wide-Mouth-Plastic-Jars)) are a fallback, but they may need their own lid disc size.

**Frosting.** Glass takes frosting spray well. On PET, use a **frosted vellum or tissue liner** inside the jar, since spray frost adheres poorly to PET. That also unifies the look between glass and PET jars.

**Gentle-tap firmware** ([DD-1](./DD-1-Trigger-Mechanism.md)) reduces shaking force on either material.

## Final Decision
**Mix by age, with the same lid assembly:**
- **Cousins 12 and under:** shatter-resistant **PET** regular-mouth-compatible pint jar.
- **Cousins 13-16:** **Ball glass** regular-mouth pint jar.

All jars get a frosted vellum/tissue liner for a consistent glow, and all use the same custom lid disc + regular-mouth screw band. **Action:** confirm the cousin age split, and confirm that the chosen PET jar accepts a regular-mouth band (buy one test jar first).

## Explanation
- **Item 15:** the youngest, most vigorous shakers get a jar that can't shatter.
- **Item 14 / item 1:** teens get real glass, a keepsake that doesn't feel like a toy.
- **Item 17:** PET survives drops. Glass for older kids, plus gentle-tap wake, keeps the risk low.
- **DD-7 compatibility:** one lid design for everyone, as long as the PET jar takes a regular-mouth band.
- **Item 3:** blended cost ~$2.12/jar (assuming a 6/6 split), still small against the item 3 budget.
