# Asset: 6080002

## Description
Design specification for the Firefly Jar: renderings, functional and non-functional requirements traced to MuSCoW, architecture, pin map, behavior state machine, bill of materials and cost assessment, firmware configuration, assembly procedure, test plan, deployment schedule, rollback plan, and the House of Quality. Each design decision is its own asset (6080003-6080012).
